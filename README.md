**Starshard Arena** is a neon arena survival game for the desktop browser. You are the Kid — spiky hair, red headband, and a blaster almost as big as you are — alone against the Hollow: eight kinds of creature, each drawn as a character with its own face and temper. Maws that chew their way toward you ringed in teeth, Lances that lean back and flash before they lunge, Broods whose unborn children drift inside a translucent sac, one-eyed Mites, cloak-and-wisp Wraiths whose mouths open a breath before they spit, bone-plated Bulwark knights that turtle when hurt, wasps whose stingers charge light before they fire — and every fifth wave the Dreadnought, a many-eyed leviathan in counter-rotating siege rings that enrages at half health. Everything on screen is bigger now: no enemy is a coin-sized sigil, and the boss fills a third of the arena. Kills drop more than points: the Hollow leave **skill Cores** — Overdrive, Seeker Missiles, Piercing Bolts, a Nova Burst — that you steal by running over the wreck, mid-fight. But the swarm eats too: a Hollow that reaches your dropped shards first swallows them and grows. Chain kills into a combo to charge the screen-clearing **Starfall**, dash through danger and leave a burning ion wake, fight inside gravity **rifts** that drag friend and foe alike, and between waves spend your shards at the Forge, where every permanent upgrade — extra cannons, piercing bolts, a seeker rack — is bought with the remains of the creatures you broke, or banked toward something bigger. Every run builds a different blaster. Keyboard and mouse, procedural WebAudio sound and music, no build step and no network: the whole game is the static files in `game/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and click LAUNCH. (To package the entry instead: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.)
